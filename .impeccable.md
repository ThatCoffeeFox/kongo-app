# Kongo design context

## Product

Kongo is an AI Japanese-learning companion. Sensei conversation is the home and primary learning loop; lesson cards, quizzes, and image reading support that loop. The MVP targets learners from JLPT N5 through N1 on desktop and mobile, including local-model use.

## Audience and needs

- Learners need a low-pressure place to ask questions and practise Japanese at their level.
- They need to understand what they can do next without learning the app's navigation first.
- Japanese text, readings, examples, and tutor explanations must remain readable at normal viewing distance and on phones.
- Local model readiness and local data behavior should be clear without making infrastructure the focus.

## Brand and visual direction

- Matte Fox identity: warm washi surfaces, charcoal ink, restrained fox orange, matte borders, and Japanese typography.
- Voice: warm, precise, encouraging, and direct. Sensei is a capable tutor, not a mascot-heavy game.
- Preserve Japanese script and ruby readings as first-class content. Prefer legible line lengths and generous line height over dense dashboard metrics.

## Product principles

1. Keep the conversation visible and central; companion tools should be available in context.
2. Make every visible control do what its label promises; never use placeholder activity or fake history.
3. Keep primary navigation labeled and reachable by touch, keyboard, and screen reader.
4. Make empty, offline, and first-run states explain the next useful action.
5. Use responsive layouts that preserve important learning context, with sheets/drawers on small screens.

## Implementation constraints

- Keep the existing React/Vite app and local-first IndexedDB path working.
- Avoid adding a large Japanese font bundle without confirming package size and subset behavior; use a deliberate Japanese-aware font stack and improve sizing/line-height.
- Favor accessible semantic controls, visible focus, and 44px touch targets.
